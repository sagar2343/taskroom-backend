Features

Everything you need to run field teams

Built for managers who need accountability, not just scheduling software.

📍

Live GPS + Route Trace

Real-time employee location with full route history. See every step, every ping, every route taken — all on the map.

Real-time
📋

Multi-Step Task Flows

Break tasks into ordered steps. Each step can require photo proof, signature, or GPS location check before proceeding.

📸

Photo Proof of Work

Employees capture photos at each step. Evidence is instantly visible to managers. No fake check-ins.

✍️

Digital Customer Signatures

Collect on-screen signatures from customers. Attached to task steps with timestamp and GPS confirmation.

🏠

Room-Based Teams

Organise employees into rooms by project, city, or department. Managers control every room independently.

📅

Attendance Management

Automatic punch-in/out with session tracking. Export to PDF or Excel for payroll in one click.

📊

Productivity Scores

Weekly A–D grades per employee combining task completion and hours worked. See your top performers instantly.

🔔

Push Notifications

Employees notified instantly on task assignment. Managers alerted on overdue tasks and step completions.

💳

Self-Serve Billing

Upgrade your plan directly from the dashboard. Razorpay-powered checkout. Invoices and history always available.
